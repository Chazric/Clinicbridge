ClinicBridge AI — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · RED REISHI LLC · redreishilife+clinicbridge@gmail.com

Summary

ClinicBridge AI stores all clinical and clinician information only on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. RED REISHI LLC does not operate servers that receive, store or process patient information, and we do not sell, share or analyse your data. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you.

Clinic licences. When a clinic licence key is entered, the app sends only that licence key and a random install identifier (generated on the device, not linked to the hardware or to you) to the RED REISHI LLC licence server, which replies with a signed licence. No patient, encounter, clinician or ledger information is ever sent. Clinic subscriptions are purchased on our website and processed by Stripe, Inc.; RED REISHI LLC receives the billing email and subscription details but never full card numbers. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Information on your device

  • Clinician profiles, patient encounter records, translations, tasks, handoffs and the audit log you create.
  • The encryption key is held in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. Your PIN is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash.
  • Data is excluded from iCloud/Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used only while you press the microphone button in the interpreter, to turn speech into text. Speech recognition is performed by the operating system's speech service (Apple Speech / Google speech services); depending on your device settings and language, the platform provider may process audio under its own privacy terms. Enable on-device recognition in system settings where available. Audio is not stored by ClinicBridge.

Translation

Translation runs on the device using Google ML Kit. The first time a language is used, its translation model is downloaded from Google; text you translate is not sent off the device.

Biometrics

Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock is handled by the operating system. ClinicBridge never receives biometric data.

Sharing and export

Data leaves the device only when you choose to export a FHIR bundle or an encrypted backup through the system share sheet. You are responsible for sending it only to destinations your organisation authorises.

Retention and deletion

Closed encounters are purged automatically after the retention period you configure (default 7 days). You can delete an individual patient or erase all data, which destroys the encryption key. Uninstalling the app deletes all data.

Healthcare organisations

When deployed by a hospital or clinic, that organisation is the data controller / covered entity and its policies apply. ClinicBridge is intended for healthcare professionals; it is not directed to children.

Demo data

The demo workspace contains fictional synthetic patients only. Do not enter real patient data in demo or test builds.

Changes and contact
