Getting started

  1. Install ClinicBridge AI from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Choose Load synthetic demo to explore with fictional patients, or set up your own clinician profile.
  3. Set a PIN; optionally enable Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock.

Licences and billing

  • Buying: clinics subscribe at clinicbridge-billing.onrender.com: $49 per clinician per month or $490 per year, with a 14-day free trial. Hospitals and health systems can ask for enterprise pricing.
  • Activating: in the app open Settings → Licence → Enter licence key, and paste the key shown after checkout (format CB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Each device uses one seat.
  • "All seats are in use": remove the licence from a device you no longer use (Settings → Licence → Remove licence from this device), or add seats under Manage subscription.
  • Changing seats, updating your card, invoices or cancelling: use Manage subscription with your licence key.
  • Offline use: a licensed device keeps working offline; it re-checks the licence when it is next online.
  • Lost licence key: email us from your billing email address and we will resend it.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your device, encrypted. RED REISHI LLC never receives patient information. See the Privacy Policy.

I forgot my PIN. For security, the PIN cannot be recovered. After repeated wrong attempts the app locks for increasing periods. If you cannot unlock it, reinstall the app; this permanently deletes the data on that device unless you have an encrypted backup.

Moving to a new phone. Create an encrypted backup in Settings and restore it on the new device, then enter your licence key there (remove it from the old device first to free the seat).

Translation quality. Translation runs on the device and is general-purpose, not validated for medical use. Always check the back-translation and preservation warnings, use teach-back, and use a qualified human interpreter for consent, serious news and whenever there is doubt.

Is ClinicBridge AI a medical device? No. It is a clinical communication and documentation-support tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace clinical judgement.
